Proven by packages/server/test/docupload.test.ts: "deletes one whose bytes do not hash to its name, and logs it", "keeps one whose bytes hash to its name, marks it verified, and never reads it again", "hashes at most SWEEP_HASH_BYTES a run and leaves the rest, a mismatch included, unhashed for the next run". Why not live: it needs a document posted on chain whose stored bytes differ from its name, and this suite posts none of its documents on chain.
- NOT RUN live: C9-registry-code: the relay checks the registry's code against the deploy record and answers every relay 503 registry_mismatch, moving no counter, when the address holds other code or none. Proven by packages/server/test/relay.test.ts: "answers every relay 503 registry_mismatch and moves no counter when the registry address holds other code or none, or the code cannot be checked", "targets the registry recorded at deploy, with the code hash recorded there". Why not live: it needs other code at the registry's address, which only a redeploy could put there; every live run checks that code hash itself before its first attack.
- NOT RUN live: C23-per-48: the relay, the demo join and the sponsor all count an IPv6 client through the one countClient rule: per /64, and every /64 of one /48 together at 4 times the per-IP limit, so the 81st request from one /48 is refused. Proven by packages/server/test/ratelimit.test.ts: "counts per IP under limit's own keys and per IPv6 /48 at 4 x perIp, with no total, and fails closed", "count all 65,536 /64s of one /48 together, refused after 4 x perIp, and leave other /48s alone", and packages/server/test/relay.test.ts: "counts every /64 of one IPv6 /48 together at 4 times the per-IP limit, so the 81st relay from one /48 is refused", and packages/server/test/demo.test.ts: "counts joins per IPv6 /48 too, so 81 /64s of one /48 hit the limit at 4 times the per-IP figure", and packages/server/test/sponsor.test.ts: "counts requests per IPv6 /48 too, so 81 /64s of one /48 hit the limit at 4 times the per-IP figure". Why not live: the dev server listens on 127.0.0.1 and takes the client IP from the socket, so this suite can only speak from IPv4 loopback addresses; IPv6 has one loopback address, so no second /64 of a /48 exists to send from.
- NOT RUN live: C23-budget-per-48: the per-IP byte and token budgets, document tokens and envelope upload bytes, also count every /64 of one IPv6 /48 together at 4 times one IP's budget. Proven by packages/server/test/ratelimit.test.ts: "counts every /64 of one IPv6 /48 together at 4 x max, so 10 /64s get at most 4 x one IP's document tokens or upload bytes an hour". - NOT RUN live: C22-upstream: an upstream redirect, a body over 1 MiB or not JSON, the 8-second timeout, and no client header forwarded. Proven by packages/server/test/proxy.test.ts: "refuses an upstream redirect without following it", "refuses an upstream body over 1 MiB and a body that is not JSON", "gives up after 8 seconds with 504", "forwards no request header: Cookie, Authorization and X-Forwarded-For stay behind". Why not live: the upstream is QoreChain's public REST, which this suite does not control and does not attack.
- NOT RUN live: C40-model: sixteen properties of the relay, the demo bot and the document path, checked after every command of randomised runs against the real handlers and stores. Requests overlap at every store and chain call, the chain can be slow, lose a transaction, lose or falsify a receipt, or have its nonce used by a foreign key, and the store loses replies on most writes, increments included, with hour and day boundaries crossed. Among them: every handler answers; counters match the distinct actions handed to the node; no join removes anyone; admission marks stay true and none outlives a dead add; spend, shares and byte budgets hold; a write retried after a lost reply never understates a ledger; one action is in flight per nonce; a join refused for a full board or a busy bot leaves nothing behind. Proven by packages/server/test/model/invariants.test.ts: "every handler answers and none throws", "each per-board, per-chair and valid hourly counter holds no more than the distinct actions handed to the node in its window, and no fewer than those handed over there for the first time", "no join removes anyone, even one that meets a board another join filled while it waited for the lock, and every removal is a sweep's or a pressure pass's, of a guest", "every admission mark belongs to a member, a guest once seated, or an add still pending, and none survives a sweep once its add is dead", "every day's booked spend covers the real fees of what was handed over that day by the world clock, and is never negative", "the relayer halts exactly when a foreign transaction used its nonce, and never otherwise", "post bytes booked equal the verified sizes of posts handed to the node; no kept document differs from a posted cipherSize; every posted document and referenced envelope survives every sweep; token bytes stay under their daily caps", "once every transaction handed over at a board nonce is unknown to the chain or reverted, neither a different action there nor the same bytes again is refused 409", "a write retried after a lost reply never makes a ledger or byte budget show less than was booked and not given back", "two different actions at one board nonce, or two copies of one action, are never both handed to the node while the first is pending", "a join answered board_full or demo_busy reserves no demo relay, takes nothing from the day, sends nothing and leaves no mark; one that met a board already full takes no hourly join and keeps its challenge", "no removal takes the demo board below DEMO_GUEST_CAP minus DEMO_FREE_SEATS members unless its guest was due anyway: six hours old, or with a KEM key gone wrong", "each share of the day's spend, as the code booked it, stays at or under its ceiling", "a relay that moved the per-board, per-chair or valid hourly counters handed a transaction to the node, or was refused by one of those counters", "a dead add's mark never refuses its guest", "a guest never sent an add is never refused already_admitted or admission_unsettled, unless another join of it ran at the same time". Why not live: it drives thousands of orderings, lost replies and clock edges that a live run would meet only by chance; in packages/server it runs with npx vitest run test/model.
