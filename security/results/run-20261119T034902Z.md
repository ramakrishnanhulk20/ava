## Proven offline, not attacked live

- C25-leak-halt: a relayer window spent outside our own sends halts the relayer for the UTC day and closes the window. Proven by packages/server/test/window.test.ts: "halts the day and closes the window when the chain shows one more transaction or 1 uqor more than we sent"; "on a halted day opens nothing, and still closes a live window it finds". Why not live: triggering it live would stop the relayer, and the demo with it, for the rest of the day.
- C22-upstream: an upstream redirect, a body over 1 MiB or not JSON, the 8-second timeout, and no client header forwarded. Proven by packages/server/test/proxy.test.ts: "refuses an upstream redirect without following it"; "refuses an upstream body over 1 MiB and a body that is not JSON"; "gives up after 8 seconds with 504"; "forwards no request header: Cookie, Authorization and X-Forwarded-For stay behind". Why not live: the upstream is QoreChain's public REST, which this suite does not control and does not attack.

## C-numbers with no live attack here

- C11: fresh epoch keys are randomness inside @ava/core's newEpochKey; nothing outside can push on it or see it; core's tests cover the source.
- C14: one key and a fresh nonce per document happen inside encryptDocument; not observable from outside; core's tests cover it.
- C18, C19, C20, C21: browser invariants (stored identity, CSP, rendering, strangers' boards); the app is not built yet.
- C26: the browser treats a relay hash as a claim until the receipt; the app is not built yet.
- C31: showing state is shared needs two server instances or a restart of the dev server, which this suite does not control; C27-second-request and C43-replay show the once-only records hold across requests.
- C32: its proof is a grep of the built app's bundles, pages and server-component payload, which do not exist yet.
- C37: the demo chair's key is attacked through C33 (no signing oracle) and C36 (the drill key); its rotation step is the runbook's own dry run, which reads the demo chair's phrase and so stays outside this suite.
- C38, C39: the exposure ledger runs in the browser; not built yet.
